    About meteocat

    meteocat compares the same days or seasons across every year since 1985, for any place in Europe. It's a non-commercial hobby project.

    How to read the numbers

    All values come from the ERA5 and ERA5-Land climate reanalysis: a weather model fed with decades of observations. Each value describes an area of roughly 10–25 km, not one weather station, and uses the same model for every year so years can be compared fairly.

    Small, short events such as tornadoes, hail or a single thunderstorm are often too local to appear. The data is provided as is, without warranty. Don't use it for safety, insurance or legal decisions; for those, ask your national weather service.

    Privacy

    • No cookies, no tracking, no ads. Nothing about you is sold or shared.
    • Visitor statistics are counted with GoatCounter, which doesn't use cookies or store IP addresses. It only records anonymous totals: page, country or region, browser type and where the visit came from.
    • Saved in your browser: weather already loaded (so it opens faster next time) and your recent places. It never leaves your device. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes it.
    • Services your browser talks to: to show the weather, your browser sends the place and dates you pick to Open-Meteo (Switzerland). Place search goes to Open-Meteo and to Photon by komoot (Germany). Like any website request, these services see your IP address. "Use my location" asks your permission first, and your position is only used to look up the weather.
